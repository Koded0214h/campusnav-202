## 📘 README.md

### 🚀 CampusNav — Smart Campus Navigation for Students

CampusNav is a campus-specific navigation platform that helps students, freshers, and visitors easily locate and navigate key places within a university environment. Built on top of Google Maps, CampusNav adds student context, localized naming, and community insights that traditional map services often miss.

---

### ❓ Problem Statement

Navigating a university campus can be confusing, especially for:

* Freshers and new students
* Visitors attending events or exams
* Students moving between lectures with limited time

Most campuses are poorly represented on traditional map services:

* Buildings are generically named or unlabeled
* Student shortcuts are missing
* Indoor and pedestrian navigation is limited
* No student-specific context exists

This leads to wasted time, confusion, and stress.

---

### 💡 Solution

CampusNav bridges this gap by combining **Google Maps services** with **student-generated campus intelligence**. It provides:

* Accurate pedestrian routing within campus
* Student-friendly place names
* Community ratings and reviews of campus spaces
* Fast routes between commonly visited student locations

---

### 🗺️ Key Features (MVP)

* 📍 **Campus-only map view** powered by Google Maps
* 🔎 **Search by student-recognized names** (e.g. “LT 2”, “New Hall”)
* 🚶 **Walking directions** optimized for campus movement
* ⭐ **Ratings & reviews** for lecture halls, libraries, hostels, and facilities
* 🏫 **Popular student locations** highlighted for easy access

---

### 🔗 Google Services Integration

CampusNav integrates multiple Google services:

* **Google Maps SDK** — base map and visualization
* **Google Places API** — location data and place details
* **Google Directions API** — pedestrian routing
* **Google Sign-In** — secure student authentication

---

### 🤝 Community-Powered Data

To ensure accuracy and scalability:

* Students can suggest new places
* Locations are verified through community confirmation
* Ratings improve routing and recommendations over time

This reduces manual mapping and allows the platform to evolve naturally.
